Privacy Policy — XAUUSD Bot

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Contact: chanmeki420@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, provided by Google when you sign in with Gmail. This identifies your account.
  • MT5 account details: the broker name, MT5 server and MT5 login (account number) you enter, plus your MT5 master password. Your password is stored encrypted on our server and is never shown back to anyone; it is used only by the bot to log in to your MT5 account and place trades.
  • Payments: the USDT transaction ID (TXID) you submit and the wallet address it came from, used only to verify your $20/month payment on the TRON blockchain.
  • Trading activity: trades the bot places on your MT5 account (ticket, symbol, direction, lot size, prices and profit/loss), so we can show you your trade history.
  • Session cookies: a cookie that keeps you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the service: to sign you in, to run the trading bot on the MT5 account you connected, to verify your payment and manage your license, and to show you your trades. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

Payment verification looks up your TXID on the public TRON blockchain (via TronGrid). The live gold price comes from Yahoo Finance (with a Kraken fallback) and the chart is provided by TradingView; those services may see your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing chanmeki420@gmail.com. Removing your MT5 account from the dashboard stops the bot for that account. Note that payment records (TXID, amount) may be kept for accounting purposes.

Security

MT5 passwords are encrypted before storage and are never returned in any response. The site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so never share your MT5 investor password or your Google password with anyone, including us — we will never ask for them.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
